All cached section pages pass the combined strict collector, and every prior record and chapter manifest remains unchanged in the final expansion.

Ten indexes list no sections: 152, 203, 255, 280, 312, 318, 342, 460, 560 and 564. These are explicitly reviewed empty indexes, not missing work. Transfer destinations 643 and 625 are included. Gaps in chapter numbering do not imply missing catalog entries.

The final two date exceptions preserve source header dates separately from a conditional latest-start deadline and an explicit footnote effective date. No controlling enactment or unprinted end date is invented.

## Definition and remaining scope

Coverage means headings of every chapter and source version listed in the checked Missouri Revisor statute catalog, with explicit handling of empty indexes. It excludes statutory body-text search, constitutional provisions, court rules, regulations, local ordinances, federal law and a complete historical archive. Historical date search and version comparisons must not imply complete history without an appropriate archive.

Physical Android performance measurements, screen-reader testing, independent alias review and held-out relevance evaluation remain outstanding. Full-text search, if added, should remain a distinct mode so body mentions do not obscure heading matches.
