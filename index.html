Coverage, dates & sources

    Coverage includes the chapter indexes in Titles I–XLI. Expand the chapter list above to see the imported scope.

    All 468 chapter indexes in the statewide Revisor catalog checked November 13, 2026 are accounted for. Court rules, regulations, constitutional provisions, federal law, local ordinances and complete historical versions are outside this heading collection. Chapter 300 is Missouri’s Model Traffic Ordinance; its inclusion does not establish which provisions a locality has adopted. A missing result does not mean no relevant law exists.

    Effective dates describe source versions. Source-check dates describe when a page was retrieved. The index build date describes this search file; it is not a publication date or a guarantee of current law.

    Searches stay in your browser. No third-party analytics or search service receives your query.
