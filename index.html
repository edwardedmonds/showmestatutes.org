Coverage, dates & sources

This pilot covers Chapter 565 only. It does not cover all Missouri criminal law, and it is not a complete historical archive. A missing result does not mean no relevant law exists.

Effective dates describe source versions. Source-check dates describe when a page was retrieved. The index build date describes this search file; it is not a publication date or a guarantee of current law.
